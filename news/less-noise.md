---
title: Less noise
date: 2026-11-06
slug: less-noise
---

Agents run longer now, and nobody likes watching one wiggle.

Star is dense. A typical session is many agents, many tool calls, many screens updating at once. That can be great — fast, legible, productive. It can also be a lot.

We're trimming wherever we can without losing the useful bits: the prompt, the edits, the diffs that actually matter. If something is happening, show it. If it's just motion, don't.

This is in progress. The first pass is quieter sessions and fewer things fighting for attention at the same time. The next passes are about making the stuff that stays easier to read.

Less noise. More signal.
